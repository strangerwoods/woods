# Childlike

#### 22:00 2026/04/04

&nbsp;

I don't understand a lot of things, nor do I want to, I've been pretty content in my own little bubble of ignorance and sadness,
I know very little about how the world around me works or how I'm supposed to interact with it, I live in my own head and no one
has access to it, no matter how close you will ever try to get to me I will forever be so distant from everyone in my life and 
there's nothing much I can do for it. I will never understand you my dear friend, not the struggles you go through on the daily
not the beautiful things you have going on in your life, to me it's everything the same, a weird shade of gray splattered with 
some hints of blood. I wish there was somewhere in the middle we could meet but I'll forever be on my own side, no matter how 
hard I try the planet will only spin one way for me, the stars will always be the same ones as ever when I look up into the 
night sky, the ever so vast fields will always be infinite and so clear for me to walk in.

It's ironic, there are no stars in the sky tonight, I don't think I've ever seen a sky full of them in all of my life, not that
I bother to chase for it of couse. Soon I will be asleep in another world of dreams, one perhaps more peaceful than this one, I
will try to think of beautiful things so perhaps I might see them when I'm deep into it.

The moon will never be the same after tonight, goodnight people

\- Stranger
