# Diary to Sadness

#### 21:00 2026/04/04

&nbsp;

Have you ever met some amount of suffering that could never make you feel the same again? some sort of truma or piece of media
that could bring you to the deepest depths of human despair? I've been looking for something like this for a while, mangas 
haven't been able to satisfy this need of pain and comprehension no matter how deep I look I still am not able to truly empathize
with any of the characters, it's like I'm on a whole different level than the worst of abuse and suffering that a person can
go through in their lives despite me being in a pretty comfy situation actually, I don't have much to do in my life I'm not hard
pressed into having to deal with any hurtful situation in particular but it still somehow feels like I'm at the bottom of the 
pit. Could it be victim mentality? do I feel like I have to carry the burden of humanity on my back which is why I'm so sad and
pathetic all of the time?

I don't know how to reconcile these feelings, I'm a loser who could get better and get out of his current predicament but how am
I able to face the reality of things, that there are people who have had it much worse than me, who suffered a hundred times as
much as I ever did yet they managed to achieve greatness, how do you look someone who got raped by their family members in the 
eyes and tell them "oh yeah I can't do it".

I'm in the deepest well on planet earth surrounded by ropes I have to climb to get out, but I don't want to get out at all 
because I'm afraid of what could be at the top, of what could be out there. So I sit down here in a pool of my own tears wailing
about how hard it is to do anything else.

I feel tired, not the kind of tired you feel after a hard day of work no no, I'm existentially tired, I'm tired of being here 
and having to pretend that I somewhat enjoy it, day after day people come people go but I don't connect with any of them, I wish
I could meet someone who's at the same level of sorrow as me and that I could ask them how to get out of it, I wish I had someone
to share my struggles with just getting up in the morning or having to get out of the house but no matter who I meet I feel 
inferior, like I'll never make it out and I'm staring up at someone who will eventually grow up to be a responsible and 
respectable individual worth something in this world, do people even have intrinsic value of their own? I produce nothing and 
give back nothing, I don't know where to go in this darkness of mine, I don't know what love is, I never experienced once in my
life, it all felt like I was trying too hard to make something fake work, it feels like I'm in a play directed by someone who
is not so good at writing.

There's this deafening sound whenever there's what you call "silence" around me, it's like the bells of damnation are ringing
for me, a spiral of madness and purgatory everywhere all at once, plaguing my brain and synapses every second that I'm not 
suffocating the noise with music or videos or anything of that kind, the space that is meant for meditation is taken up by 
death's alluring voice that creeps ever so closer to me. The sky is opening up and the tears of the angels are raining upon me
yet another day that I don't feel like being part of this mortal plane anymore. 

My fingers rest on my keyboard not knowing what else to say what else to scream into the void of nothing, how many people who 
have already died have left behind a blog to narrate their existance or to be known for it. I wish I never existed in the first
place. Why do I have to do all of this ungratifying work every day to try and get a message out, that I'm fucking suffering 
and I see no way out of it. Why do I have to even try?
