# Voices from the afterlife

#### 21:53 2026/09/02

I hate not knowing everything about you, down to your deepest fears and concerns about who you are and what your 
objective in this world is, I really wish we met under different circumstances, maybe in a freer world, maybe in a place
that felt familiar to the both of us, but sadly we've found ourselves in this messy and unbecoming hellhole that we 
can only leave through means of death, perhaps once we're both in the ground and rotting we'll finally get the chance 
to be honest to eachother and understand what our feelings really mean, perhaps we were not meant for this way of 
existing, and maybe we will finally and truly, be one forever and ever again. I feel like you've already heard me speak
of this over and over again but death rocks, it is my personal beliefs not necessarily dictated by any religion or 
anything similar that there's an afterlife for us, one with true meaning and a bigger objective than we can ever imagine,
it is simply pitiful that we have evolved for billion of years, growing with fear of death and the unknown only to be 
left with this feeling of not being enough, of not understanding enough, we're all alone, all of us, on this little 
planet of ours, surrounded by limitless space and a forever expanding universe, I yearn for more and you'd think that 
if we prayed long enough at least some being out there would answer us but no, we're alone, we've been left to our own
devices and abandoned by whatever is, or isn't out there. I have truly never felt this lonely until this very moment 
in my life, I would pray to any creature that is lenient enough to let me exist without desire, without having to worry 
about bullshit money and relationships and all the burdens that come with being a human social creature. I hope and pray
that we will meet again, under different circumstances. With love.

\- Stranger
