# Suicide Note

#### 20:12 2026/03/23

&nbsp;


This is not my actual suicide note but I've been thinking about writing one someday, when I finally decide to end
things for good, I'm not grateful for much in this life, not the friends I made along the road not the people I 
lost and found, because none of them lasted. Maybe that is the point of life, the impermanence of the material 
world or however the **Buddhists** see it but it just doesn't sit right with me, not having someone in the end
is a tragedy worthy of the greatest masterpieces of this world, it's been a hard life and it still is, no matter
how much effort I put in something I still end up feeling like I have no skill at all in it.

I have no one to say sorry to, I'm not gonna blame my death on anyone nor do I have to make up any excuses for it,
this world just isn't for me and it hurts having to be reminded of it on the daily in every task I have, I lack
the motivation or drive that creates geniuses or disciplined people, I am also not insecure to the point of having
to make up for it by working 10x harder than other people.

I won't miss anyone and that's pretty sad, not because I hate people to the point of not wanting to remember them
but because I simply do not have the connections necessary to feel that kind of emotions towards someone else, it's
not my fault nor anyone elses it just had to be this way fron the higher forces that gave birth and brought me 
into this **messed up and pointless world**. To die for a cause would've at least meant for something to me but 
I do not have the following nor the interest to pursue some sort of suicide cult that venerates the ending of one's
life. 

I sit around my room, listening to music, shitposting to strangers online and that's all I have to show for it,
a big nothingburger of pathetic self-commiseration (if that's how you spell it). 

This is all from me, hopefully in another life we shall meet as different people.
