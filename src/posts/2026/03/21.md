# Fleeting emotions

#### 20:12 2026/03/21

&nbsp;

Yes yes yes yes I know I know getting better is cool and all and you should feel good about it and whatnot, *however*, 
losing your mind can become a sort of addiction, getting used to daily monotonous life of productive routines can lead you
to lose yourself a little bit, not in the way that you can't find yourself but in the way that you can't find what makes 
you interesting anymore, it felt good for a while, being on the brink of destruction, being the person who's on the edge
of despair reaching to never before felt peaks of human misery it's an extatic feeling, but then you start to mellow down,
responsabilities start catching up to you and you become a shell of your former miserable self, and you have to start 
findind what makes you **you** again, I don't think a rebirth as a people loving introverted nerdy guy fits me it's somewhat
far from my actual thoughts on people, on the other side of the coin I could become a productive cynic, someone who does 
what it takes but at the same time still holds a grudge against ***all of humanity***.

I've been reading before going to sleep, usually after getting done with studying, It's been good so far I just don't see
where the plot is trying to go, I miss reading more slice of life psychological horror mangas they were much more 
stimulating in my brigade for the discovery of the human mind.

I'm still iffy on reading actual books, like, I used to do it a lot before but I'm scared that I'll lose myself in reading
and distract myself from the real world, to which I am very grounded to right now for some reason, possibly the new meds
I'm taking are starting to kick in and making everything more real to me, more physical.

I still struggle with my perfectionism and strugging with feeling like I'm not doing enough, my impostor syndrome creeping
up whenever I learn something new and I still wonder if I'm actually understanding what I'm doing or if I'm just typing
like a robot just reading back on what I was taught, I don't understand what "learning" is in general, is it just memories?
is it a change in how you do things? is it something more abstract that rewires how your brain understands reality? I'm not
sure, I need to investigate more despite most of my experimentations usually not yielding much results.

Dinner is almost ready so I have to leave things as this for now, I shall read more before going to sleep and think more
about things tomorrow after more studying.
