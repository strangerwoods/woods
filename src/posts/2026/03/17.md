#### 20:36 2026/03/17

I've studied a bit more today, it felt slightly nice but it also makes my head hurt a lot, my longing for death is the same 
as usual and I don't have much to say, I went for a visit at my psychiatrist to consider going to the psych ward but since 
I've been able to focus even a little bit on my studies I've decided to pospone it, I don't know if I'll be able to reach 
the end of them, life still doesn't feel meant for me, maybe it's love that it's lacking or human connection but it still 
just doesn't feel right, eternal rest just sounds and feels so nice to be in. 

I've been thinking of writing a suicide note but I don't know what I would write in it, I don't see anyone to be sorry to 
or anyone who I would feel guilty of depriving of my presence, I don't even think there's anything more left unsaid, I tried,
I tried a whole bunch but it never felt right or worked out for me, I experienced a lot of things that even older people 
haven't, maybe the abundant drug abuse grew me older or something but it feels like I've been on this planet too long.
The me right now who's writing this is so different from who I was before I started going drugs and having all these overly
suicidal and painful ideas.

I dream of space, of being all by myself on my own planet, self sufficient and alone, I do not wish to have anyone else 
around me. Living among humans is probably the worst experience I have ever had the displeasure to be in, all gross and weird
looking sacks of meat, It's recurring theme in my thoughts how much I dislike them, even using the word "us" feels unnatural
in this context, I am not like them, I do not want to, I was born to be a fucking rock or something, anything, less alive a
bit more akin to an object, they don't have to struggle with existentialism do they? does the fucking rock you kick around
on the way to your school, job, car ever have to ask itself what it's purpose or what the meaning of it's existance is, does
it have to feel horrible because the other rocks around it don't like 'em, because they're different? No they fucking don't
that's what's so great about being a fucking unmoving piece of stone. 

I find myself crying when looking at supposedly inspirational videos, not the david goggings braindead youtube short kid
garbage, but actually phylosophical and meaningful videos that try to address the actual concerns and struggles and ideas
that plague the minds of those stuck in pain and suffering, it hurts to watch because I feel powerless to fight against 
the pain and all the damn sadness that perpetually floods every inch of my cranial cavity.

Money money money, I think I'd be happier if I was rich and didn't have to care about getting a job (yea yea unemployed and 
whatnot, you know you'd be happy too if you didn't have to slave away on the daily), I'd still be in my room doing my little
room things but it would take away all the pressure from the outside world, even if from the outside it seems like I live 
without caring those small things still frustrate me because they're in the back of my mind at all time, I don't have the 
luck of being born in a rich family and able to live simply off of my family and inheritance for the rest of my life so I
have to eventually get my shit together, and that's what keep me from sleeping at night.

I'll leave you with this for now, perhaps next time I write I'll have a more positive outlook on life, perhaps the meds I 
take would've done something to help me as it feels like they do nothing, no like seriously, I started convincing myself 
that medical drugs are all fake bullshit made to just make people feel like they're there to help them while instead it's
snake oil.

This has been all from me today.
