# Overflowing

#### 00:16 2026/03/07

&nbsp;


There's no way out for me is there, I gotta admit I've done a lot of perverse and inhumane things in life, things
I do not wish upon other people and that I regret doing wholeheartedly, if I could turn back time I would do things
maybe a bit differently, I would either care much less about whatever I had going on at the time or I would put in 
much more effort into things, but as it stands right now I am beyond fucked in this limbo of having accomplished 
every little and having wasted much more than I could affort.

I'm thinking about starting things over with therapy, getting admitted for a long ass time in the psych ward to take
a break from everything that is going on in my life and try to reconnect with who I used to be when I was younger
but I'm scared, really scared that things won't work out in there and it's this fear that prevents me from taking
any possible action to come around and fix all of my problems.

When I was younger I was really into space and the universe without even realizing it, it was mesmerizing to me 
to the point where I wished I could travel it aboard my little space ship and visit stars and new 
planets of the sort, admiring them from as close as I can get.

My meds are really helping to calm down my thoughts and keep my mind away from harmful ideas, I haven't cut in so
long because not even the blade makes me feel anything any more, sure it's a light sting but my thoughts are much 
stronger and invasive than it. The idea of dying together with someone else crossed my mind a whole bunch of time 
in recent times but I never really found that *someone*, likely because I'm a shut-in borderline **neet** who 
doesn't really connect with people all that well, never had any real relationships to speak of or the "normal"
teen experiences that everyone else goes through with aging so I never developed the same "reactions" that my
peers go through when a certain something happens around them.

Someone called my rambling from the depths of my heart the "baring of the soul" but I don't truly understand 
what that really means, to me being as honest and straightforward as possible comes natural, proably because 
I never got to learn how to properly handle social cues and conversations with others ever since I was younger,
autism partly to blame for it since it somewhat reduces my perception of what is going on around me and the gravity
of things.

I watched people cry over bad things happening in their lives but never really understood them or was able to 
empathize with their current situation, the hardest part is seeing your own family members cry or bother you over
something that is considered **important** yet not being able to grasp what they actually want or why they're 
behaving the way they are, it's really lonely in here isn't it.

I never really developed a musical "taste" or any sort of nieche interest that was only about me and that I could 
ramble on to people on end, I learned a bit of everything just to be able to keep up with other people are saying,
to make small remarks here and there and have an opinion on what everyone else is saying without actually caring
much about what comes out of their mouth holes or what their stance on things is, I've always done just me.
It's a pretty lackluster way of living and somewhat dishonest but I feel like I don't have much to say on a lot of
topics, **c'est la vie** would be my reply to a lot of things, people seem to love to argue to death over the 
smallest of things that can't be changed or who have a clear and transparent resolution yet they refuse to 
acknowledge it because they're afraid it'll take something away from them.

It's getting pretty late so I should go to sleep soon, it was nice to be able to come to terms with these parts
of my being, it's still a long road before I make the decision to end my life or finally bring things back around
so this is all from me for tonight, goodnight people, have lucid dreams and get higher than the sky itself.
