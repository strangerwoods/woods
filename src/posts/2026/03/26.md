# Drugs

#### 21:36 2026/03/26

&nbsp;

I guess it's been long overdue.

My first experience with drugs was weed at some point in highschool, I don't even really remember my first time I just
remember that it suckeeddddd, I was unaware of my autism and depression and whatever I have going on in my mind so it used
to make me go paranoid as fuck every single time I smoked, it was not a pleasurable experience but it did make me somewhat
calmer and more laid back at times so I guess it wasn't the worst.

And then, oh and then I found molly/shrooms, my first time was taking both at the same time when I was at home alone doing
nothing much interesting, the shrooms were disgusting cause I was just chugging them raw, molly tastes ass too but the 
happiness I achieved in that single moment was the best experience of my whole life, truly I would give up anything to go
back to it.

my second hard drugs experience was psychadelics, LSD to be specific even though mushrooms are too. At the time I didn't know
what the normal dosage was nor did I care enough to follow it so I eyeballed a good dose (3/6 times higher than what is 
usually recommended for first time users) and just rolled with it, I ended up "passing out" on my bed for what felt like
about 3 hours, it felt like I was dead for a while, it's probably the experience that made me grow into having little fear 
of death and actually longing for it, it felt peaceful, relaxing, once I snapped out of it and woke up it was like I was 
reborn. 

I've been an avid user of psychadelics ever since and enjoyed pretty much every moment of it, I feel no shame in writing it 
down because it's the truth, those were the best moments of my life and drugs are the only things that can bring them back.
It felt good you know, the feeling of seeing more in the world than there is, it felt like a release of all the pent up
energy and dissatisfactions I ever had, I don't even know how much longer I can go without getting my fix, hell perhaps I'll
even go back to my old job that I hated just for the sake of having more money and buying more drugs, it's not a good idea
or thing to do I know but it just feels like this is what I'm alive for, to feel more than I'm normally able to.

This isn't a warning message, hell maybe it's even an invitation to go and try them, but the only realization that I had from
my many experiences I had with them is that they're gonna make you feel great, and they'll be the last time you ever feel 
this way on your own.

I love drugs, I love everything about them and I'm not sorry for it, nor am I ever gonna try and be the guy who goes around 
public places to tell people to stay away from them or whatever, please be my guest and try them.
