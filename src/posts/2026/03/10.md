# Spasms

#### 16:32 2026/03/10

&nbsp;

My new meds are making me go more insane than usual, there's these weird zaps that start in my brain and make my
whole body twitch it's so fucking disgusting I don't know what to do with them, I need to start seeing demons not
become physically disabled and distorted. I feel like I'm losing even more hair than usual due to stress, I manage
to get absolutely nothing of any importance done.

Every muscle in my body feels like it's not mine, I want to break it off and make it all stop it feels so goddamn
heavy and **unnatural**, I think I'm slowly losing more and more control over everything, I'm nothing but an empty
husk pretending to be alive and moving but on the inside I'm rotten beyond belief, once you take down the walls I
have put up for so long I'm falling apart, body and mind as one, I even have to remind myself that I'm a human once
in a while otherwise all these experiences feel like they aren't mine, I'm guessing it's either dissociation 
or derealization? I need to look more into both 

> Dissociation is a broad term describing a mental process where a person feels disconnected from their thoughts, feelings, memories, surroundings, or sense of identity.  It can occur as a normal response to stress, trauma, or fatigue, and is often temporary. 

> Derealization is a specific type of dissociation characterized by a feeling that the external world is unreal, dreamlike, foggy, or distorted.  People experiencing derealization may perceive people, objects, or environments as lifeless, artificial, or unreal, even though they know this perception is not accurate. 

I'm not sure which of these two is the correct one for me, or if I have either of those at all, sometimes it feels 
like a lot of time has passed and I need a second to recognize my surroundings and realize that I'm still alive and
breathing, that the world is still there.

I can feel myself getting closer to death every second of my life, everything is feeling less and less real, I can
hardly make out what surrounds me anymore, it's like everything is just an automatic action from me rather than 
something I actively think about doing or engaging in, even sitting in my chair writing this feels so disconnected
and surreal to me. My words become insignificant and a garbled mess of nothing.

I thought writing was gonna mean something to me, that it was gonna help me sort out my ideas and feelings but at 
this point it feels like I'm writing more of a will or a memoir for when I'm dead, tales usually have a moral at
the end, some sort of warning sign for people to be wary of in life, is this what my tale is? a warning not to do 
too many drugs, to not slack off to not think too much about certain parts of life.

I'm a bit pissed still, that it all had to happen to me, I never had many bad things happen to me in life, not the
sort of thing outside of your control that ruins you or people who do you so wrong that you spiral into a bad train
of thought. It's more like I was destined for this, to feel god awful all of the time and not have anything in
common with my peers other than stuff I was forced to like going to school or basic social interactions in those
environments.

My reactions to things that don't work out have gotten more violent and self destructive, I don't think I have found
myself enjoying what I do in ages. I haven't been cutting lately but I'm thinking of starting it again simply for 
(the love of the game (kidding)) the sake of it, so I have something that can ground me to the real reality and 
keep me from losing my mind over not being able to feel anything at all. 

I think I'm starting to lose the plot a little.
