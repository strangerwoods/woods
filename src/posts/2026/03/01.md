# Inconsistent

#### 23:03 2026/03/01

&nbsp;

It's been uhh, pretty hard to label the emotions I've been feeling lately, not because I feel the need to
but because I need to justify it to the outside world, am I just an autist am I slowly going insane are my
thoughts taking a mind of their own because of the copious amount of drugs I did in my past. I am truly
lost within the universe and I don't know if I should cherish this ability to feel in the first place or if
I should resent it for the unanswered questions it brings with it.

&nbsp;

I wasn't always this way you know, when I was younger I asked myself a bit less questions, with age came
the existential crisises, not something that a child should ever go through but alas the world opened itself
to me way before I was ready for it, I know nothing nor have I ever learned anything new it feels like my brain
is just hard wired to be this way no matter the age or situation, I'm always just me, never changing.

&nbsp;

I've been uh, fantasizing about suicide more lately, more than usual, it's almost as if it's the only solution
to finally bring me peace now, among the other meatsuits I don't belong, I belong in a place much better than this.
Everything has been hurting for so long now I don't remember a time when I wanted to actually achieve something, the
days have become dull and gray blending into eachother as if I never sleep, it's like a string that never ends.
I feel like ripping my own skin off, digging into my brain just to see what lies at the bottom of the human soul,
I wanna ask my inner self what it is he wants so bad to have, what it's lacking, I truly hate the self within the self,
it's a constant battle, a contradiction that can never be solved or come to terms with.

&nbsp;

Who even am I, am I the outside shell or the inner observer, am I the one who is writing all of this down or
the one judging if what I'm writing is worth anything at all. I never had much luck in love because I can never
trust a being I can't dissect until I unwrap them, boundaries are a pain and I wish there was someone out there
who is as open as me, not trying to make it a moral stance of course, I clearly understand where they come from
and what their purpose is but I just can't bring myself to care about them because they aren't part of me.
