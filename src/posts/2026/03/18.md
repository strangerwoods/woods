# Doubts

#### 19:02 2026/03/18

&nbsp;

Do I really want to get better, what if I quit my meds randomly and decided to spiral into unending despair, have you seen
how many people die on the daily, one day I could just be a number in that list. The therapy group I was gonna go to is no
longer a thing for now, that's pretty depressing, I wish I could have more people to experiment with, mess around and try
to figure how they react to the ways I act.

I feel very uninspired today, I had a bad moment while studying which made me pretty mad and overall just put me in a 
horrid mood to do anything, I might try again more tomorrow but my confidence that I had 2 days ago is wavering.

I've always had this weird attraction to serial killers, not in the fanboy/girl way where you think they're so great and
inspiring or whatever no no none of that garbage, but their struggles, their mental logic that leads them to do certain
acts of violence and rebellion against their god awful lives, you can't stop but think that maybe just maybe you have 
something in common with them, they're human too after all, in the same way you can relate to a movie villain or a really
evil book character. The moment I stopped looking at other people as if they have any worth other than what they provide
to me in terms of happiness, enjoyment, entertainment is when I started doubting if maybe, just maybe, I share something
with people who have committed the most violent and heinous acts you could ever see or hear of.

I find myself living from checkpoint to checkpoint, from meal to sleep to waking up the next day, I'm really tired of not
being able to live in the moment, I wish it would all end right now.

I'm starting to lose the plot of what I'm writing, I'll try to finish my homework soon enough and hopefully I'll actually
get something done, I'm gonna cry myself to sleep tonight
