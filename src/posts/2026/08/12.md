# Untold Love Story

#### 16:28 2026/08/12

&nbsp;

I dream of the perfect love, the one that makes your heart skip a beat when you meet the person of your dreams, 
someone who can save you from the bottomless pit of anxiety and unreciprocated feelings that makes you rot from the 
inside no matter what you do to try to get out of it, there has to be a person out there who can make me feel like 
I'm a complete person again, I've been leaving pieces of me with whoever came into my life up until now but no one 
ever stayed with me at all, I yearn for that special someone who can bring life and shine into my life so I don't have
to suffer anymore, I seek the kind of love that makes you wanna die for the other person over and over again until 
there's nothing left of you anymore, I want to blow my brains out while looking at her in the eyes just to show how much
I care about her, she emits this comforting and bright light from all around her, it kills me from the inside to know
that I will never be able to even reach a tenth of how magnificent and inspiring she is, there's so much beauty in all 
of her actions and emotions, you can just tell she's the most perfect girl in the entire world but I'm just a loser
who can't get close to her without stuttering or slurring my words because I'm oh so scared of missing my one chance 
in this lonely world, I hope she kills me with her gaze and splits my head open with an axe, she's so precious and I 
wanna die at her hand like the useless being that I am, I used to think I couldn't stoop lower but every day I find 
a new way to make it so I hate myself even more and I hope she sees me for the lowly creature that I am and takes pity 
on me just so I can feel like a person once again, to be seen by someone whom you love is truly the most ecstatic 
experience one could ever fathom to experience, her love is like a grace from god himself onto a sinner like myself, 
how I wish she would even look in my direction just so I can dream of her for another night. I wanna be choked to death,
hang by my neck, have my throat slit, my eyes gauged, I want to be tortured by the love that has always been within me
for the last few weeks, I'm so tired of disappointing everyone that is around me and the last hope that I have left is
that at least someone will finally see me for who I am, not the caricature of me that I had to come up during all these
years, I wanna be honest for someone for the first in my life and be accepted for who I am, I wanna receive love and 
be able to give back as much as I can without the fear of it going wasted, I really hope that such a person is out there
and that they'll find me one day, because god knows how few days I have left in me before I decide to give up on being
someone's special person.


\- Stranger
