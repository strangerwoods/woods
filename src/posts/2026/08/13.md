# To die for you

#### 21:40 2026/08/13

&nbsp;

Sometimes I wish I was a different person, a different face a different personality, completely different mannerisms, I
wish I could be the one who wins at life, the one who finds true love and is able to move on past mistakes and forget 
things easily, I wish I could live without you in my life, the words that I speak make less and less sense the more I 
think of you in my life, the shadows creep up on me and whisper my name, I hope that some day they'll invent a solution
to the problem of being me, I keep chasing dreams that cannot ever be reached, I cry myself to sleep at night thinking 
about what it could've been, I'm in love with the feeling of being in love. I wake up looking at my phone hoping that 
someone realized I'm not okay and they want to fix me somehow, they say go to therapy, engage in more activities with 
your peers, stimulate yourself, but what does it take to heal a broken person who doesn't care about such little things
in this world, I just want to rip the skin off of my face and kiss you while slowly bleeding out, I wanna die in your
arms and forget all about everything that has ever happened in my life, I wanna reach the heavens knowing I did the 
right thing and loved to the fullest, you're more than the eye can see, there's a hidden star inside of you just waiting
to come out, perhaps not in this life, maybe in the next. I painted the walls with my own blood just for god to take 
notice of my sinful self and smite me from reality within a nanosecond, there's just so much I wanna say to you and 
things I wanna talk about all day long till my throat dries up and closes forever. I want to be something I'm not and 
yeah I suffer because of it but it's okay because I will be dead before I reach 24 and my ashes will be thrown out into
a river that no one cares about, all that I am and will ever be is stuck in this small place that's my entire world, I
will never see the light of day no matter how much I try, Goodnight.

\- Stranger
