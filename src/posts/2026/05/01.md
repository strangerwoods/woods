# Slow descent into insanity

#### 21:21 2026/05/01

&nbsp;

I feel the need to die closer and closer day by day and I'm starting to give into it, what if there is another life past 
this one, one much better than the previous one, hell it doesn't even have to be better, maybe just different, is this 
what escapism is like? Yet it feels like something much more than just that, it's like a passion for the unknown, nothing
in this world satisfies me except the possibility of having so much more further in life, or rather outside of it. 
The days have been boring and unimpressive, severely monotonous and borderline laughable at, I do nothing I learn nothing
I enjoy nothing I never enjoyed anything everything lasts for a few seconds and then vanishes into absolutely nothing. 
What I came up with in these 22 years of life is a single thing: die, you might enjoy it more than you think. 

I feel absolutely nothing, tomorrow could be the worst day in my whole life and somehow it would amount to the same as the 
day before. I yearn for nothingness and that is who I am, that is my motto, I'm a stain on the big painting that is 
humanity. 

My posts have gotten shorter and so has my interest in being someone.
