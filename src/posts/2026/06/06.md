# Massacre

#### 21:30 2026/06/06

&nbsp;

So, I haven't written here in a while haven't I, I've been busy coming to terms with my own mortality in the last 
weeks, what I expect from death and what awaits for me in the afterlife, I feel for the first time in a while I can
empathize with the religious folk, that sort of belief you must have in order for your reality to make sense and for
it to not be as brutal as it usually is, I don't care that much about finding a purpose or having to do anything in 
particular to keep me going anymore, drugs are a distant dream too right now, escapism through death is all that I 
have left anymore. I am weirdly okayish, like a heavy burden has been lifted from me, I am ready to die, I can feel 
death on my shoulder softly whispering to me about the wonders of the life after this one and it's really enticing, 
it leaves me wondering why so many religions disavow suicide or simply don't talk about it in their texts, maybe 
occultism would fit me better, my own religion which grants you an eternity of bliss no matter what you've done in life
, all the monstrous things you've ever done or thought of all a distant bad dream, I can feel closer to heaven just
by talking about it, I'd be a great cult leader, for the small amount of people that plan on killing themselves.
I don't seek just peace anymore in the afterlife, I wish for adventure, redemption, freedom and all the such, it's a 
really beautiful thing if you ignore the fact that well, you gotta be dead for it, but this world isn't the only one we
have, not anymore, with this new set of beliefs at my hand I can finally reach the place that I've always longed for 
but that was unavailable to me due to my young stubborness, by the end of the year I'll probably dead and it's a 
relaxing thought, I am free at last from all the "have to do"s.

Perhaps in the next life, as different people or exactly the same as now we will meet again and go through things in a 
different way, for the both of us. Always love you, Stranger.
