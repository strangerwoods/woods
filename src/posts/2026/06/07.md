# Affinity for death

#### 21:30 2026/06/07

&nbsp;

Have you ever felt so in love that your heart could explode inside of your own body with how good everything and 
everyone around you feels, like you just solved life's biggest problem and found your reason to keep on existing 
forever and ever more, I'm starting to feel it the closer I am to the end of my days, I am really happy lately, 
I'm slowly gathering the courage to end my own life and reach the peace I longer for so many days and sleepless 
nights. Enough with the theatrics for now, to put it simply I'm fine, I take a deep breath and feel lighter for 
once in my entire life, my blood grows colder and colder day by day but my heart is finally at ease with how the 
world is, I can finally admit to all of my sins and wishes and oh god I am finally free. You know, I always wanted
to have my own story, to feel like the main character for once, I know it sounds edgy and childish but I don't 
think I ever moved on from that stage in my life anyway, I'm still the same young daydreamer, this world is both
too big and too small for me, there's simply too much going on in it for me to be who I want to be and do the 
things I want to do, mistakes, achievements both come with repercussions for which I am not ready for, I don't 
really regret the things I've done, the people who got involved with me received what was bound to happen when 
interacting with a cynical and mentally unstable person and I will never see myself as bad or evil, I really 
enjoyed my time here for as much as I could but I am at the end of the road, there's nothing left for me to do
or try, I simply cannot manage to win in a place such as this. I'm truly at the peak of my happiness in this moment
and I will try to make it last until the moment my soul leaves my body and wanders the universe for a better place
to exist in, for now this is everything from me, with love.
