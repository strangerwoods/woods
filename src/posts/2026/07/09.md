# Making sense of humans

#### 13:44 2026/07/09

&nbsp;

I have this friend that I somehow struggle to understand a bit, she's smart and she can manage to do whatever she 
sets her mind on, but she has these hobbies and addictions that I can't quite make sense of, like, if you're so 
capable what is it that stops you from achieving greatness and being fully sober, straight edge and managing all your
emotions without the need of something else. She recently got a tattoo to cover up old scars and that's really 
impressive, I did the same a few years ago. What I'm trying to say is that I'm really fascinated and inspired by 
people like her, who manage to move on from the pain that was in their life at a certain point in their life, yet 
I can't move on, I'm stuck in this place no matter what because I lack the strength that they, she has. I must say
I'm very impressed, which is something you can rarely hear from me as I'm very strict with compliments and praise.
I guess I'm a bit envious of her lowkey, she's great and I wish I was more like her in some ways, I'm simply 
infatuated with her ability to change things around and make them work her way, maybe it's not this way at all 
but it certainly feels like she's getting things done out of her own strength rather than things just working out
for her magically, which is admirable. At some point it felt like somewhat staring into a mirror of my own mistakes
and the things that I could've done better myself, it's scary to see someone with your own mindset out there in 
the wild and it's even scarier seeing them change into a better version of themselves, it's like watching someone 
finally becoming a human for the first time in their life, and it's a really precious moment that should be 
remembered for a long time. In summary what I wanna say is that I'm really proud of her and I wish I could be like 
her even if just a bit and that she should never give up now that her road to living an amazing life has opened up
even if just by a bit more.
